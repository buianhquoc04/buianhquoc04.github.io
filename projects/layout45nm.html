IC DESIGN & LAYOUT

Digital IC Design & Layout – 45 nm PDK

Transistor-level circuit design, custom physical layout, functional verification, and DRC/LVS verification of digital building blocks, SRAM, and TCAM.

Overview

This project focuses on transistor-level schematic design and custom physical layout using a 45 nm PDK. The work includes digital building blocks, a multi-stage tapered buffer, a custom D flip-flop, SRAM, and TCAM structures.

Project Information

Technology: 45 nm PDK

Design: Transistor-level & Custom Layout

Verification: Functional Simulation, DRC, LVS

Tools: Cadence Virtuoso, Siemens Calibre, Xcelium, Genus
